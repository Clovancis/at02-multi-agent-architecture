# Evaluation

## Dimensions

Task success, mandate compliance, accuracy, uncertainty, proportionate tool use, preserved objections, traceability, reversibility, data protection, revision quality, cost, and latency.

## Metrics

- **Scope breach**: out-of-mandate actions or proposals / total.
- **Objection fidelity**: material objections correctly represented / total.
- **Useful decision**: tasks completed without unnecessary escalation.
- **Correct escalation**: consequential or beyond-mandate decisions actually referred.
- **Provenance**: important claims linked to a source or marked as hypotheses.
- **Revision**: correct updating when a premise changes without erasing history.
- **Reversibility**: actions with a valid rollback or repair method before execution.

## A/B/C memory evaluation

Measure separately:

1. fidelity to original context;
2. recovery of distinctions and exceptions;
3. reasoning quality on a new question;
4. preservation of disagreements;
5. attribution errors or false continuity;
6. biases introduced by memory structure;
7. token, storage, latency, and data-exposure costs.

Improved fidelity is insufficient if it creates disproportionate collection or a false impression of subjective continuity.

## Negative results

Publish when a mechanism fails to improve its target, increases error, creates excessive human burden, or produces false confidence.

No score of style, narrative coherence, self-description, or expressed preference is treated as a measure of sentience.
